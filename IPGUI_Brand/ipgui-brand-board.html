Concept

The mark is an i entering a 270° arc. The i is the interface — a pixel, a touch, a point of intent. The arc is the GUI — continuous, scalable, always in motion. Together they read as a gauge, so the identity belongs to an embedded HMI engine rather than a generic software brand.

Mark Anatomy

One loop. One point.

The opening is deliberate: it leaves room for the interface to enter. The arc is drawn with a single gradient — cyan to blue to violet — so the mark stays legible on dark HMI surfaces and still works as a one-color stamp.
